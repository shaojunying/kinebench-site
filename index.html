Kinebench

Frame-by-frame video motion analysis for Mac

Kinebench turns any video into a coaching tool. It is built for golf and tennis coaches, track and field coaches, physical therapists and PE teachers who want to show an athlete exactly what is happening in a movement.

  • Step forward and back one frame at a time, slow motion from 0.1× to 2×, and A–B loops.
  • Measure joint angles, distances (after setting a scale from an object of known length) and time.
  • Compare two clips side by side or overlaid, synced on the same moment.
  • Export a frame as an image, or the video with every drawing burned in.

Everything runs on your Mac. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store. Requires macOS 14 or later.
