Kinebench

Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Kinebench is a video analysis app for macOS made by Junying Shao. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: Kinebench does not collect any data.

Data we collect

None. Kinebench has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting services, and it does not send anything over the internet.

Your videos and drawings

Videos you open stay where they are on your Mac; Kinebench only reads the files you choose. The drawings and measurements you make are saved inside the app's own storage on your Mac so they come back when you reopen the same video. Exported images and videos are written only to the location you pick. None of this leaves your Mac unless you share it yourself.

Third parties

Kinebench contains no third-party code that collects data. If you download the app from the Mac App Store, Apple may collect information as described in Apple's own privacy policy; we do not receive personal information from Apple beyond the anonymous sales and usage reports Apple provides to all developers.

Children

Kinebench does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
