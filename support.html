Getting started

  • Open a video with File ▸ Open Video (⌘O) or drag it onto the window. Not sure where to start? Choose Open Sample Clip.
  • Step with ← and →, or ten frames at a time with ⇧← and ⇧→. Space plays and pauses; − and = change the speed.
  • Press [ and ] to mark the start and end of a loop.

Measuring

  • Angle (G): click the end of one limb, then the joint, then the end of the other limb.
  • Set Scale (K): drag along something whose length you know, such as a 1 m stick or a hurdle, and type its length. Distances then show in real units.
  • Distance (D): drag between two points.
  • Timer (T): click where it should appear; it counts from the current frame.
  • Choose the Select tool (V) to move drawings or their end points. Delete removes the selected drawing, and ⌘Z undoes.

Comparing two clips

Switch to Compare (⌘2), open a clip on each side, move each one to the same moment (for example ball contact) and click Sync Here. With Linked on, both clips then play and step together. Choose Overlay to see one on top of the other.

Exporting

Use the Export button or File ▸ Export to save the current frame as an image or the video with your drawings burned in. If you set a loop, you can export just that part.
